Supported Hardware

Wokwi simulates a wide variety of hardware components, including microcontrollers, sensors, displays, and more. It supports the following architectures: ARM, AVR, RISC-V, and Xtensa.

Microcontrollers​

The following microcontrollers are currently supported:

FamilyMicrocontrollers
AVRATmega328P (Arduino Uno, Arduino Nano), ATmega2560 (Arduino Mega), ATtiny85
ESP32Xtensa: ESP32, ESP32-S2, ESP32-S3
RISC-V: ESP32-C3, ESP32-C5*, ESP32-C6, ESP32-C61, ESP32-H2, ESP32-P4, ESP32-S31*
STM32STM32C031, STM32L031, STM32F103C8
Pi PicoRP2040 (Raspberry Pi Pico), a dual-core ARM Cortex-M0+ microcontroller

* ESP32-P4 support is in beta, ESP32-C5 and ESP32-S31 support is in alpha.
